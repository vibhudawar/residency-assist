# A previous residence visa is still active (check R9)

**Sources (checked 2 Oct 2026):**
- u.ae, general provisions for the residence visa — https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/Visa-information/general-provisions-for-the-residence-visa [official]
- ICP, residence cancellation and grace periods — https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e68 [official]
- MOHRE, cancellation of work permits — https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/cancellation-of-work-permits-and-employment-contracts-2022 [official, from search summary]
- GDRFA Dubai, cancellation of residence permits — https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/services/0613ab0e-5858-11ea-0320-0050569629e8 [official]

## Why it blocks the new application
- A person can hold only one UAE residence at a time. If an earlier residence visa is still active, the **new one can't be issued until the old one is cancelled**. [guidance]

## Who cancels it
- **Only the sponsor can cancel a residence visa.** The applicant can't cancel it themselves. [official]
- If the old visa was for **work in the private sector**, the previous employer first cancels the work permit and contract through **MOHRE**; the employee signs to confirm they received their dues. [official]
- In Dubai, the residence itself is cancelled through GDRFA Dubai's **"Cancellation of all types of residence permits"** service (GDRFA website or Amer centres). [official]
- Family members sponsored by someone must be cancelled **before** the sponsor's own residence. [official]

## What the assistant says when R9 fails
- "Our check found that a previous UAE residence visa is still active. That needs to be cancelled before the new one can be issued, and only the previous sponsor — usually your previous employer — can cancel it."
- "Once they've cancelled it, the application can continue."
- Create a follow-up (tool: `create_followup`, rule R9).

## Grace period after cancellation (only if asked)
- After a residence is cancelled or expires there is a **grace period** to leave or change status. It **depends on the residence category**, from 30 days up to 180 days. [official, ICP]
- Do not tell the caller which grace period applies to them. Say: "It depends on your category — the cancellation document shows the exact last date, or you can check with GDRFA Dubai."

## Do not say
- A specific grace period for this caller, or that they are already overstaying.
- Anything about the previous employer's obligations beyond "they need to cancel it".
