**Note:** none of the 12 checks in this prototype is about attestation. Use this only if the caller asks why a certificate was needed, or mentions a certificate problem.

## What attestation is
- Certificates issued abroad must be **attested** before they are used for a UAE visa:
  1. by the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the country that issued them**, then
  2. by the **UAE Embassy or Consulate** in that country,
  3. then by the **UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs**. [official]
- For some professions, a university degree also needs attestation by the **UAE Ministry of Education**. [official]
- Documents must be in **English or Arabic**, or come with an official translation. **Laminated documents are not accepted.** [official]

## Which certificates
- **Work visas:** usually the degree or qualification certificate, depending on the profession. [official]
- **Family sponsorship in Dubai:** an attested **birth certificate** for children and a certified **marriage contract**. [official]

## Where to do it
- UAE MOFA services are online: **mofa.gov.ae**, the **UAE MOFA app**, and UAE missions abroad. [official]
- Some countries offer **digital attestation**, one application for both the embassy and UAE MOFA, using UAE PASS. Only in some countries. [official]

## What the assistant says
- Explain the steps above in plain words and point to mofa.gov.ae. This can't be done on the call.
- Offer to create a follow-up or a callback if the caller needs a person.

## Do not say
- That digital attestation is available in every country.
- Fees or processing times, except that MOFA states most digital attestation requests take about 2–3 business days.
