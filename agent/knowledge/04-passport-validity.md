# Passport validity (check R8)

**Sources (checked 2 Oct 2026):**
- ICP, issuing a residency permit — https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e64 [official]
- u.ae, preparing to work in the UAE — https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/employment-in-the-private-sector/preparing-to-work [official]
- GDRFA Dubai, amendment of residence data — https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/services/dff87d9f-b81d-11ed-5210-4cd98f768936 [official]

## The rule
- The passport must be **valid for at least six months** to issue a residence permit. ICP: "Passport valid for no less than 6 months." [official]
- GDRFA Dubai services also require more than six months of passport validity. [official]

## What the assistant says when R8 fails
- "Your passport expires in less than six months. A residence visa can't be issued on it, so the passport needs to be renewed first."
- "Passports are renewed by your own country, usually through your **embassy or consulate** in the UAE. Once you have the new passport, the application continues with the new passport details."
- This is not something the assistant can fix on the call. Create a follow-up (tool: `create_followup`, rule R8) so a person can track it.

## After the new passport arrives
- In Dubai, moving residence details to a new passport is handled by **Amer centres** (GDRFA-approved service centres) or the GDRFA Dubai website. [official]

## Do not say
- How long passport renewal takes, or what it costs — that depends on the applicant's country.
- The fee for moving the visa to a new passport — say "check the official fee".
