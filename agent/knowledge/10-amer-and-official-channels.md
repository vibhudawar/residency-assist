# Amer centres and official ways to check an application

**Sources (checked 2 Oct 2026):**
- GDRFA Dubai, contact information — https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/contact-information [official]
- u.ae, track visa application and validity — https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/Apply-and-track-visa/track-visa-application-and-validity [official]
- Dubai.ae, check visa validity and track an application — https://www.dubai.ae/living/residency/check-your-visa-validity-and-track-your-application [official]

## What Amer centres are
- **Amer centres** are **GDRFA-approved service centres in Dubai**. They handle visa and residency transactions in one place: entry permits, issuing and renewing residence, cancellation, moving residence to a new passport, visit visas and establishment services. [official]
- Walk-in customers take a queue ticket; appointments are arranged when needed. [official]

## Official contact for Dubai residency questions
- **Amer call centre: 800 5111** (toll-free inside the UAE). From abroad: **+971 4 313 9999**. Email: **amer@gdrfad.gov.ae**. [official, GDRFA]
- For the other emirates, ICP: **600 522222**. Don't confuse the two. [official]

## How an applicant can check their application themselves
- **GDRFA Dubai website** — status enquiry with the application number, transaction number and payment date. [official]
- The **GDRFA Dubai app**, an **Amer centre**, or **800 5111**. [official]
- The **DubaiNow** app also shows residency status. [guidance]
- In this prototype, the applicant also has a **private status link** to the Residency Assist portal.

## Use this when
- The caller asks "Is this real?" or "How can I check this myself?" — point them to the channels above. Never give a number or website that isn't on this page.
- The caller needs something this call can't do (anything beyond this application) — suggest Amer or 800 5111, or offer a person.

## Do not say
- How many Amer centres there are, opening hours, or fees — say "check with Amer".
- That Residency Assist is part of Amer or GDRFA. It is a separate prototype.
